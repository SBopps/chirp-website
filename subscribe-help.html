Subscribe to Chirp

Subscriptions are managed from inside the Chirp mobile app. Here’s how to get there:

  1. Open Chirp on the phone where you signed up.
  2. Sign in as the camp administrator.
  3. On the lock screen (or Settings), tap Subscribe Now.
  4. Enter your card on the secure Stripe checkout page.
If you don’t have the Chirp app installed yet, search for “Chirp” in the App Store or Google Play.

Stuck or can’t get to your phone? Email hello@chirp.camp and we’ll help you get reactivated.
