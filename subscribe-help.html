Manage your Chirp subscription

To manage your Chirp subscription, open the most recent billing email from us and click the button inside — it includes a secure link to your camp’s billing portal.

Can’t find the email? Email hello@chirp.camp from the address associated with your camp and we’ll send a fresh link.

Opening your billing portal…

You’ll be redirected to Stripe to manage your subscription.
